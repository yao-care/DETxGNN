Detailed, verified mechanism-of-action data for lonafarnib is not available in this evidence pack (original MOA field is a data gap). However, contextual information indicates lonafarnib acts as a **farnesyltransferase inhibitor**, blocking farnesylation of Ras and other host-cell proteins — a pathway relevant to its approved use in HGPS and its investigational use in hepatitis delta virus infection (which relies on host farnesylation for viral assembly).
